About Us

Introduction to Skinalyze

Skinalyze is an AI-powered skincare recommendation system designed to personalize skincare solutions based on your skin type and needs. Whether you're struggling with acne, dryness, or simply want to maintain a healthy glow, Skinalyze is here to guide you with tailored advice.

Our Mission or Purpose -

We aim to eliminate guesswork in skincare by using AI to provide scientifically backed, customized skincare advice. Too often, people rely on generic products that don’t address their unique skin concerns. Skinalyze solves this by offering personalized routines to suit every individual.

How Skinalyze works -

Skinalyze leverages advanced ML to analyze your responses to a simple quiz about your skin type, concerns, and habits. Using machine learning and expert dermatological rules, it analyzes the skin's condition and provides recommendations with real-brand products, ensuring effective and practical recommendations.

Our Unique Selling Point

Unlike generic skincare tools, Skinalyze uses advanced machine learning algorithms and real-time skin analysis to deliver highly personalized results. Our focus on combining cutting-edge technology with dermatology-backed data sets us apart, making your skincare journey both unique and reliable.

Who Are We -

Developed by Mugdha Pawar, Bhumika Nikam, Mahee Pol and Manasi Agale ; students at Government Polytechnic Mumbai, with a passion for AI and skincare innovation. This college project reflects our dedication to merging technology with practical solutions to enhance everyday life.

Our Vision for the Future

We’re excited to expand Skinalyze with more features, including deeper skin analysis, compatibility with a wider range of products, and a dedicated mobile app. Our goal is to continuously evolve, bringing the best skincare experience to our users worldwide.
